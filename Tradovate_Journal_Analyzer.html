Fixed settings and limitations
  • Unzoned timestamps are interpreted as Asia/Bangkok, based on Roland's November 12–13 CSV/chart cross-check. This is a personal profile, not a universal Tradovate default.
  • Buy/sell times establish row entry/exit and direction. Exported pnl is treated as gross. It is never multiplied again by quantity.
  • Fees use Tradeify's published round-trip schedule, checked November 13, 2026. These are estimated deductions, not verified actual account charges. Historical rates may differ.
  • Daily/weekday: Bangkok exit time. Hourly: Bangkok entry hour. All valid rows in the file are included; filename date ranges are not used to filter records.
  • Calendar dates are not journal sessions or official provider days. Account balances, campaign grouping, rule compliance and account breaches are not inferred.
  • Exact duplicate rows or duplicate normalized match keys block report export. Shared exit IDs with different entry IDs are not duplicates by themselves.
  • One account per file. If the export omits account identity, the tool cannot verify this condition.
  • Unknown instruments or non-USD fees are not guessed: gross remains available and net statistics become unavailable for the whole import.
